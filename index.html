§01 Stance

Frontier models made the model the easy part.

When models solve the mathematics, three things stay hard: stating the right problem, proving the answer on your own data, and running it where that data lives. That is what we build.

Models under your control

Any model, inside your boundary. We start with what your environment already provides, and fine-tune on your data when precision and cost justify it.

Data as the strategic asset

Governed, secured and usable. A model is the same for everyone; your data is what makes your AI different from your competitor's.

Proof before scale

A pilot measured on your real cases, with KPIs agreed up front and error bars reported. Then we industrialise what works, and stop what doesn't.

Every figure traceable to its source line

A lubricant distributor in Senegal. Satellite imagery, deep learning, mapping.

Challenge
The client sells to thousands of informal garages and workshops. No map of them exists, and a field census would take weeks.
System
A detection model fine-tuned on very-high-resolution satellite imagery finds zones of mechanical activity. A mapping app lets sales teams target, filter and track field visits.

Scale
A method repeatable city by city: Dakar, then Thiès, Touba and Tambacounda, at marginal cost.

§03 The mathematics underneath

One forecast, worked in the open.

What will an ageing asset produce over the coming years: low, base and high? A model can write the code. It does not decide what is worth estimating, or how sure you can be of the answer.

Synthetic assets, generated in your browser: 200 to learn from, the others never seen by the model. Output is relative to each asset's first month.

01 · Principal components

Find the few shapes that matter

y ≈ ȳ + s₁u₁ + s₂u₂ + s₃u₃

Two hundred decline curves all look different, yet three shapes explain almost all of them. Forecasting three numbers is far steadier than forecasting every month.

Above: 200 known assets (faint) and one unseen future (dots), rebuilt from 1, 2, then 3 shapes.

02 · Bayesian regression · Monte Carlo

Forecast low, base and high

s | history ~ N(mᵀφ, 1/β + φᵀSφ)

The first months of an asset predict its three numbers, uncertainty included. We simulate thousands of futures and read the band. An exact band exists here, so we draw it to check the simulation.

Above: history, the simulated 80% band and base case, the exact band (thin lines), and what actually happened (dots). α, β = argmax p(s | α, β).

03 · Calibration

Check that 80% means 80%

P(low ≤ y ≤ high) = 0.80, then counted

A band is only worth its coverage. On assets the model never saw, we count how often reality fell inside, and show the figure whatever it is.

Above: how often reality fell inside the 80% band. Thin line: each batch of 16 unseen assets, which wanders. Orange line: everything counted so far, which settles. Rings: the forecasts shown in the middle plot, a triangle when below 60%.

The same chain of reduce, forecast, check sits under demand, pricing and maintenance forecasting. Every figure above is computed in your browser as you read.

§04 Engagement

Three steps. Fixed scope and KPIs at each one.

No phase starts without your written go-ahead. The first step is deliberately small.

Durations drawn to scale: 1–3 days, about 4 weeks, 6–8 weeks.

  1. Step 1 · 1–3 days

    Audit & Roadmap

    Decide what to build, and why.

    • Data and architecture audit
    • Use cases ranked by ROI and feasibility
    • Target architecture and plan
    • Risks, GDPR, effort and cost
    • A go / no-go decision

  2. Step 2 · About 4 weeks

    Pilot / MVP

    A first version running on your data.

    • Model, agent or automation
    • Pipeline from data to inference
    • Evaluation on your KPIs, with confidence intervals
    • Monitoring and logs
    • Documentation and handover

  3. Step 3 · 6–8 weeks

    Industrialisation

    Your AI service in production, at scale.

    • MLOps and LLMOps: CI/CD, monitoring
    • Hosted models, fine-tuned where useful
    • Cloud or on-premise
    • End-to-end security
    • Teams trained and autonomous

Then, continuously: supervision, model maintenance, new features and scaling.

§06 Data security

Your data stays yours.

Designed for sensitive environments: customer, financial, industrial and health data. Security is set at scoping, not added at the end.

  • In your environment

    Deployed in your cloud tenant or on your servers, hosted where you require.

  • Models with you

    Open-source models can be fine-tuned and self-hosted, so no data goes to a third party.

  • Zero reuse

    Your data never trains third-party models or serves another client.

  • Access rights respected

    Role-based control and row-level security: the AI never returns more than a user's rights allow.

  • Full traceability

    Data lineage and audit logs. Every result can be justified back to its source.

  • Compliance & reversibility

    GDPR and local regulations. Code, indexes and settings all return to you.

§08 Questions

What buyers ask us first.

Our team already has access to a frontier model. Why do we need a studio?

Access to a model is not a system. What takes the work is connecting it to your data with access rights intact, evaluating it on your real cases, adding guardrails, and running it in production. That is the part we build, and the part you keep.

Do you always fine-tune and self-host models?

No. Our architectures are independent of the model provider. We use the model that fits: one already available in your enterprise environment, or an open-source model hosted with you when confidentiality, precision or cost call for it.

What do we get from the 1–3 day audit?

A data and architecture audit, use cases ranked by ROI and feasibility, a target architecture with effort, cost and timing, the risks, and a clear go / no-go. You can stop there.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Code, indexes and settings return to you, with documentation and playbooks so your teams can run and extend the system.

How do you work day to day?

One 30-minute weekly sync, one written update a week, and a formal validation at each stage.
